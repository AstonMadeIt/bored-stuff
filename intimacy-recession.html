About The Data

Sexual frequency data comes from the General Social Survey (GSS), conducted by NORC at the University of Chicago. SSRI prescriptions from Express Scripts Drug Trend Reports. Marriage data via U.S. Census sources. Screen-time estimates from Nielsen Total Audience reports and Pew research summaries.
